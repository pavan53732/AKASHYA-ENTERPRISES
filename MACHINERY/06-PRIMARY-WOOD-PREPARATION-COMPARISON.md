**DERIVED FORMULA.** The [plant mass balance](05-WOOD-MOISTURE-AND-DRYER-SIZING.md) uses 1,000 MT/month saleable output, 10% product moisture and 5% dry-solids loss. Keep purchasing intake, actual cutter feed, screen rejects, internal recirculation and dryer-accepted discharge on separate weighment lines. As a **PLANNING ESTIMATE**, use ≥6 wet MT/h demonstrated for a common 40/50% route. At a 10% oversize fraction per screen pass, a loop delivering 5.57 wet MT/h would process ~6.19 MT/h gross and recycle ~0.62 MT/h, assuming no reject disposal, unchanged moisture and steady flow. Size the loop on trial data.

Capacity arithmetic assumes the OEM rating can be compared with the required wet intake. The claim is usually for product output or an unspecified material basis. Catalogue headroom cannot qualify a machine. For a longer preparation schedule, 5.0 sustained wet MT/h requires at least ~316 productive prep hours at 40% or ~379 at 50%, before any additional losses or reserve. A schedule decoupled from drying requires a designed surge or storage arrangement and a tested restart strategy.

## 4. Match the discharge to the dryer

| Route | Interface and open engineering question |
| --- | --- |
| Direct sawdust → flash | Infinite IFD specifies **<8 mm**. The ESM catalogue's 2–10 mm and written quote's 4–8 mm bands both need sieve and elongated-particle verification against a strict cut. IFD literature has <45% and <50% inlet language, both excluding the exact 50% case. |
| Coarse chips → rotary/hybrid | Infinite RFD includes a rotary pre-dryer followed by flash. Its own pages give <12, <15 and <16 mm descriptions. Use the conservative <12 mm for provisional screening until Infinite confirms the accepted cut and particle geometry. Even the current Jay ≤15 mm band can miss that limit. RICO chip size is unknown. EcoStan RDD catalogue lists <25 mm and ≤50%, with throughput and evaporation at 50% unresolved. |
| Coarse chips → wet/refining mill → flash | Obtain a separate wet-rated size-reduction offer, or price a compatible first drying stage before fine milling. EcoStan HM literature caps feed moisture at ≤15%. Jay JK-BD literature gives ≤3 mm diameter × ≤10 mm length and has conflicting moisture claims. |
| Pre-sized clean sawmill residue | A separate inlet and metering path can bypass full-log reduction if residue meets chemistry, particle-size and moisture limits. Identify oversize, stones and tramp metal at receiving and verify moisture and source by batch. |

Tray or belt samples should record moisture by wet mass, chip length/thickness, sieve fractions, bark/fines, rejected and recycled mass, gross feed and dryer-accepted wet discharge during the same run. Compare water evaporation, outlet moisture, actual dried mass, fuel and electricity on the same sampling window. Sources: [Infinite IFD](https://www.infinitedryer.com/ifd.html), [Infinite RFD](https://www.infinitedryer.com/rfd.html), [Infinite dossier](../PROCUREMENT/SUPPLIER-RESEARCH/06-INFINITE-ENERGY.md), [EcoStan rotary](https://www.ecostan.com/products/rotary-dryer), [EcoStan HM](https://www.ecostan.com/products/hammer-mill), [Jay dryer](https://jaykhodiyar.com/biomass-dryer-machine/).

## 5. Equalize the operating and commercial comparison

| Field | What to measure or confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Feed and throughput | Representative Eucalyptus, Casuarina and Subabul batches with log Ø/length and bark distributions, separately at 40% and 50%. Log mass at intake, clean cutter feed, screened accepted discharge, oversize recycle, waste, restart and interruption losses. |
| Drives, layout and automation | Exact model and revision, GA footprint/height/weight, foundations, service and knife-lifting clearance, feed/discharge elevations, cutter and all auxiliary motor ratings, loaded rotor RPM, current peaks, starters and power factor. Record interlocks, overload response, line controls and supplier interface responsibility. |
| Energy | Meter the entire prep package, including feed, screen, recycle and discharge. Specific prep energy = metered kWh ÷ accepted wet MT. Track idle, startup and load separately. Connected/nameplate HP cannot establish energy per tonne, electrical demand or cost. |
| Wear, uptime and labour | Knife, anvil, screen, bearing, belt, roller, gearbox and hydraulic parts by SKU. Record tonnes and hours between changes, matched-set count, sharpening limits and turnaround, time to change/clean, service personnel, routine stops and unscheduled blockages. The [maintenance SOP](03-Breakdown-Risk-Mitigation-and-Maintenance-SOP.md) gives planning intervals pending OEM and trial evidence. |
| Operating cost | Compare all-in energy cost per accepted wet MT, wear-set cost net of permissible sharpenings per accepted wet MT, stoppage and labour. Track installed equipment, spares, foundations, cabling, extraction and residence-time-dependent handling separately. Aggregate annual measures across the modelled utilisation and replacements before the later 15-year comparison. |
| Evidence required | Exact written Ex-Works scope, GST, freight/insurance to Anakapalli, installed cost, FAT/SAT conditions, trial duration, output tolerance, warranty and remedies. Require supplier agreement to measured sustained duty and particle distribution before technical qualification. |
