# AKASHYA ENTERPRISES
## Machinery Breakdown Contingency & Maintenance SOP
**Facility:** 1,000 MT/Month Wood Biomass Briquette Plant, Anakapalli  
**Objective:** Prevent production halts, protect boiler delivery commitments, and eliminate single points of failure.  

---

## ⚠️ Downtime Risk & Financial Exposure

In a 1,000 MT/month factory operating 25 days/month:
* **Normal Production Rate:** 40 Metric Tonnes per day (across two 8-hour shifts).
* **Revenue at Risk:** ₹3.60 Lakh per operating day (40 MT × ₹9,000/MT).

| Breakdown Scenario | Impact on Finished Production | Delayed Revenue | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Press #1 Fails (Press #2 Running)** | Production drops by 50% (20 MT/day) | ₹1.60 Lakh / day | Factory continues operating at half capacity. |
| **Both Presses Down (3 Days)** | Complete shutdown (120 MT lost) | ₹9.60 Lakh | Severe delivery default risk to boiler clients. |
| **Primary Chipper / Shredder Fails (3 Days)** | No fresh sawdust generated | Buffer depleted | Presses must run on stored dried sawdust stockpile. |
| **Pneumatic Dryer Fails (5 Days)** | No wet sawdust can be processed | ₹16.00 Lakh delayed | Presses stop once dried buffer runs out. |

---

## 🛡️ The 5-Pillar Breakdown Mitigation Strategy

### 1. Dual 50% Capacity Press Lines (50% Production Protection)
* By choosing **two 90 mm presses** (each rated at 1.3–2.0 TPH) instead of one giant 3.5 TPH press, a breakdown on one machine never shuts down the entire factory.
* If Press #1 undergoes ram or die replacement (typically 4–6 hours), Press #2 continues producing 20 MT/day.
* The factory can schedule routine maintenance on Press #1 during the morning shift and Press #2 during the evening shift without stopping operations.

---

### 2. Strategic Buffer Stockpiles (Decoupling Upstream & Downstream)
Do not feed the presses directly in a continuous rigid line. Instead, create two buffer reservoirs:
* **Wet Chip Buffer (Outdoor Yard):** 10–14 days of shredded wood chips stored under tarpaulins. If the primary log chipper breaks down or needs blade sharpening, the dryer continues feeding from this pile.
* **Dry Sawdust Buffer (Indoor Shed):** As shown in the site visit photo `07-tractor-loader-sawdust-handling.jpg`, maintain a 2–3 day indoor mound of dried sawdust (80–120 MT). If the dryer stops for cleaning or furnace maintenance, both briquette presses can continue running at full speed.
* **Finished Briquette Safety Stock:** Keep a 3-day stock of finished briquettes (120 MT) in covered storage. This guarantees zero delivery disruption to high-priority boiler customers in Parawada / Atchutapuram during unexpected machine downtime.

---

### 4. Dedicated Maintenance Roster & Dedicated Fitters
As budgeted in the DPR manpower plan (₹8.50 Lakh/month):
* **2 Full-Time Mechanical/Electrical Fitters** (₹30,000/month each) dedicated exclusively to lubrication, vibration monitoring, die resurfacing, and electrical checks.
* **Daily Preventive Window:** 1 hour between shifts dedicated to clearing sawdust accumulation, greasing main bearings, checking oil levels in the press crankcase, and inspecting die taper wear.
* **Weekly Planned Overhaul:** Sunday / weekly off dedicated to blade sharpening, screen replacement, and electrical torque checks.

---

### 5. Contractual Supplier SLA (Service Level Agreement)
When issuing Purchase Orders to EcoStan Machineries or the selected dryer vendor, enforce these mandatory terms:
1. **Guaranteed Spares Dispatch:** Vendor must guarantee dispatch of non-stock emergency spares within 24–48 hours via express air cargo.
2. **Technical Support Response:** Dedicated service engineer dispatched to Anakapalli within 48 hours for catastrophic mechanical failures during the 12-month warranty period.
3. **Comprehensive Commissioning Training:** Supplier engineers must train Akashya Enterprises' operators and fitters on die alignment, ram adjustment, and blade sharpening before final sign-off.
