# 500 vs 1,000 MT/month — corrected owner-side engineering comparison
**22 Nov 2026; selling price ₹8,000/MT, delivered wet logs ₹2,000/MT; all figures illustrative, no land.**

| Metric | Dedicated 500-MT plant | Integrated 1,000-MT plant | Two independent 500-MT plants |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Production MT/month | 500 | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Revenue ₹ lakh/mo | 40.00 | 80.00 | 80.00 |
| Incoming wet logs MT/mo at 40% moisture | 789.47 | 1,578.95 | 1,578.95 |
| Monthly OPEX ₹ lakh | 37.53 | 68.50 | 75.06 |
| **EBITDA ₹ lakh/mo** | **2.47** | **11.50** | **4.94** |
| Fixed CAPEX ₹ crore | 3.69 | 5.20 | 6.35 |
| Separate press units | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Main wood/dryer system | Smaller shared within line | High-output **shared** | Independently duplicative |
| Press failure consequence | Finished production stops | ≈50% planned line capacity survives | ≈50% survives if other full line unaffected |
| Shared main dryer failure | Whole plant halts | Whole plant halts after dry buffer | Other independent dryer/line can continue |

Source: inherited internal cost allowances, **not** competing priced full-line vendor bids. Savings from sharing loader, weighbridge, electricity infrastructure and qualified staff explain lower cost of integrated facility. Two independent lines on one site might share those assets and change the comparison.

**Do not repeat the previous ₹14.94 lakh/month EBITDA for two × 500 plants:** it assumed selling at ₹9,000; at **₹8,000**, its corrected result is **₹4.94 lakh** under unchanged ₹75.06 lakh OPEX. The old “build 1,000 MT infrastructure / install one press” phase-1 **₹8.5–9 lakh** EBITDA was not validated at ₹8,000 and must be remodelled as a distinct project; an oversized dryer and fixed infrastructure may make first-stage 500 MT a loss.

## Expansion options to quote
1. One integrated 1,000 MT plant — lowest *indicative* CAPEX, but chipping/dryer are single-point failure risks.
2. Two truly independent 500 MT plants — higher plant CAPEX/staff with better uptime isolation, *not automatically* two loaders/weighbridges if co-located.
3. Phase 1 one press + full-size chipping/dryer, phase 2 second press — expansion flexibility but expensive at part load; must specify dryer turndown and initial power/staff cost.
4. Hybrid: common receiving/weighbridge, **dual modular drying / two presses**, plus adequate dry-wood buffer — quote explicitly.

## Required vendor guarantees
At 1,000 MT/month, 25×16 h schedule, 85% effective uptime: two presses each **≥1.471 MT/h running**, wood size reduction **≥4.64 wet MT/h**, water removal **≥1.563 MT/h** at 40%-wet-to-10%-finished moisture. Request ≥1.8–2.0 MT/h evaporative dryer package with reserve, not vague “2 TPH dryer”. Real duty and spare margins depend on tested feedstock.

**Decision:** secure 3 full-line bids for options 1/2/4, and a priced option-3 expansion schedule. Evaluate present value of 15-year operations and downtime risk, not just upfront equipment price.
