> [!CRITICAL]
> **Why 1,000 MT is Mandatory at ₹8,000/MT Selling Rate:**  
> At the verified floor rate of **₹8,000/MT**, a 500 MT/month plant is barely viable—it generates only **₹2.47 Lakh/month EBITDA** (a meager 6.2% margin) because fixed overheads (loader, weighbridge, management, demand charges) eat up almost all revenue.  
> In contrast, the **1,000 MT/month plant absorbs fixed overheads efficiently and yields ₹11.50 Lakh/month EBITDA (₹1.38 Crore/year)** at a healthy 14.4% margin. Running at 1,000 MT is the key to commercial success at this price floor.

## 🔄 Three Expansion Paths Evaluated

### Option A: One Integrated 1,000 MT Factory (Recommended for Maximum Profit)
* **Configuration:** 1 high-capacity log chipper (5 TPH) + 1 large 1,800 kg/hr dryer feeding 2 × 90 mm mechanical presses.
* **Machinery Budget:** ~₹3.10 Crore | **Fixed CAPEX:** ~₹5.20 Crore.
* **Monthly EBITDA:** **₹11.50 Lakh** (at ₹8,000/MT floor).
* **Pros:** Lowest initial machinery cost per tonne, highest EBITDA, lower specific power consumption.
* **Cons:** Requires full capital upfront (~₹6.19 Cr including working capital) and immediate 1,000 MT/month sales commitments.

---

### Option B: Purchase Two Separate 500 MT Plants (Not Recommended)
* **Configuration:** Duplicating everything into two parallel mini-plants (2 small chippers, 2 small dryers, 2 presses, duplicate conveyors, panels).
* **Machinery Budget:** ~₹4.24 Crore | **Fixed CAPEX:** ~₹6.35 Crore.
* **Monthly EBITDA:** ~₹14.94 Lakh.
* **Cons:** Costs **₹1.14 Crore more in machinery** and sacrifices ₹6.56 Lakh in monthly profit due to duplicated operators, motors, and lower efficiency.

---

### Option C: The Smart Phased Route — "Build for 1,000 MT, Start with 500 MT"
* **Phase 1 (Month 1 to 12):**
  * Acquire land (1.5–2.0 acres) in Anakapalli.
  * Erect the full 15,000 sq ft factory shed and 630 kVA HT transformer.
  * Install the 1,000 MT-capable log chipper (ESM 150) and rotary flash dryer.
  * Install **Press #1 (90 HD)**.
  * **Initial Investment:** ~₹4.40 Crore.
  * **Monthly Output:** 500 MT | **EBITDA:** ~₹8.50–9.00 Lakh/month.
* **Phase 2 (Month 12+ once sales contracts stabilize):**
  * Purchase and plug in **Press #2 (90 HD)** (+₹26 Lakh) and extended cooling conveyor (+₹8 Lakh).
  * Scale to 1,000 MT/month immediately without shutting down the factory or rebuilding civil works!
  * **Payoff:** Minimizes upfront commercial market risk while keeping the path open to reach ₹21.50 Lakh/month profit.
