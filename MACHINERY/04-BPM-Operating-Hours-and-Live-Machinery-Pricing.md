## ⚙️ 1. Demystifying "BPM": Model Sizing vs. Ram Stroke Speed

In the biomass briquetting machinery industry, **BPM** carries two critical engineering meanings:

### A. Manufacturer Model Code: EcoStan BPM Series
EcoStan Machineries classifies its mechanical piston briquetting presses by the **BPM** designation:

| EcoStan Model | Briquette Diameter | Published Production Capacity | Main Motor Rating | Typical Feedstock |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BPM 40** | 40 mm | 150 – 250 kg / hour | 23 HP (17 kW) | Sawdust / agro husk (Micro-scale) |
| **BPM 60** | 60 mm | 600 – 1,000 kg / hour | 47 HP (35 kW) | Sawdust / coffee husk / bagasse |
| **BM / BPM 70 HD** | 70 mm | 700 – 1,300 kg / hour | 59 HP (44 kW) | Groundnut shells / mixed sawdust |
| **BPM 90 HD (Our Core Press)** | **90 mm** | **1,300 – 2,000 kg / hour** | **89.5 HP (67 kW)** | **Hardwood sawdust, forestry residues, wood chips** |
| **BPM 100 HD** | 100 mm | 1,400 – 2,500 kg / hour | 115 HP (86 kW) | High-volume industrial wood waste |

### B. Mechanical Ram Frequency: Blows / Strokes Per Minute (BPM)
* **Stroke Frequency:** The mechanical crankshaft reciprocates the compression punch at **220 to 240 Strokes/Beats Per Minute (BPM)**.
* **Extrusion Physics:**
  * Each forward stroke rams a measured charge of dried wood powder (8–10% moisture) into the tapered steel die under **1,200 to 1,500 kg/cm²** hydraulic-equivalent mechanical impact pressure.
  * The intense friction elevates the internal die temperature to **180°C – 220°C**, melting natural wood **lignin**, which flows between the wood fibers and cools into solid, binderless 90 mm cylindrical logs.

## ⏱️ 2. Operating Hours vs. Throughput Math (1,000 MT Target)

To deliver **1,000 Metric Tonnes of finished briquettes per month** (12,000 MT/year), how many hours must the plant run?

$$\text{Required Run-Rate (TPH)} = \frac{1,000\text{ MT}}{\text{Operating Days} \times \text{Daily Scheduled Hours} \times \text{Uptime Availability Factor}}$$

### Comparison of Three Shift Operating Schedules:

| Parameter | Schedule 1: Two Shifts (Current Base) | Schedule 2: Extended 20 Hours (Recommended) | Schedule 3: Three Shifts (24/7 Continuous) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operating Days / Month** | 25 Days | 25 Days | 26 Days |
| **Scheduled Shifts / Day** | 2 Shifts × 8 Hours = **16 Hours/Day** | 2.5 Shifts = **20 Hours/Day** | 3 Shifts × 8 Hours = **24 Hours/Day** |
| **Monthly Scheduled Hours** | 400 Hours | 500 Hours | 624 Hours |
| **Plant Uptime Factor** | 85% (15% downtime for dies/maintenance) | 85% (15% downtime) | 80% (20% downtime for shift-checks) |
| **Net Productive Running Hours**| **340 Effective Hours / Month** | **425 Effective Hours / Month** | **500 Effective Hours / Month** |
| **Total Factory Output Required**| **2.94 MT / Hour** | **2.35 MT / Hour** | **2.00 MT / Hour** |
| **Number of Presses Operating** | **2 × BPM 90 HD Presses** | **2 × BPM 90 HD Presses** | **2 × BPM 90 HD Presses** |
| **Required Output Per Press** | **1.47 MT / Hour / Press** | **1.18 MT / Hour / Press** | **1.00 MT / Hour / Press** |
| **Operating Load on Machinery** | **80% – 85% Load (High Stress)** | **60% – 65% Load (Optimal Sweet Spot)**| **50% – 55% Load (Very Low Stress)** |
| **Impact on Dies, Blades & Spares**| Higher wear; dies need re-polishing every 25–30 days. | **Extended die life (35–45 days); lower motor heat.** | Maximum die life; requires 3rd shift manpower (+₹2.2L/mo). |

> [!TIP]
> **Engineering Recommendation for Akashya Enterprises:**  
> Start operations on **Schedule 2 (20 hours/day across 2 extended shifts with 4 hours of night maintenance and cooling)**. At 1.18 TPH per press, the EcoStan BPM 90 HD runs effortlessly without overheating the crankcase oil or tripping motor thermal relays.

## 📑 5. What Akashya Enterprises Must Demand in the Live Turnkey Quotation

When requesting formal binding quotations from **EcoStan Machineries** (Ludhiana) or competing manufacturers, enforce this 6-point checklist:

1. **Guaranteed Water Evaporation (Not ambiguous "3 TPH Dryer"):**  
   Demand a contractual guarantee: *"The dryer must evaporate a minimum of 1,800 kg of water per hour when drying hardwood chips from 40% moisture down to 10% moisture at rated fuel consumption."*
2. **True Continuous Output per Press:**  
   Contractual guarantee that each **BPM 90 HD press delivers ≥ 1.40 MT/hour** of finished 90 mm briquettes on continuous 16-hour runs using coastal A.P. *Casuarina / Subabul* wood.
3. **Turnkey Battery Limits:**  
   Specify whether the price includes feed conveyors, discharge tracks, control panels, internal wiring, motor starters, hydraulic oil, and foundation anchor bolts.
4. **Transit Freight & Transit Insurance:**  
   Machinery manufactured in Punjab requires ~1,900 km transit to Anakapalli. Obtain freight quotes ex-factory vs. delivered site (FOR Anakapalli).
5. **Erection & Commissioning Terms:**  
   Include vendor engineers' boarding, lodging, per-diem charges, and performance trial requirements before the final 10% payment release.
6. **Die & Ram Wear Guarantee:**  
   Written wear-life guarantee for punch rams (minimum 800–1,200 MT before hardfacing/replacement) and dies.
