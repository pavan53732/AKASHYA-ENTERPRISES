## 📌 Executive Summary

This repository contains the comprehensive research, technical sizing, financial cost model, and Detailed Project Report (DPR) for setting up a commercial wood biomass briquette manufacturing plant in Anakapalli, Andhra Pradesh.

The plant processes untreated wood logs, offcuts, and branches using an integrated size reduction line, rotary drum industrial drying system, and heavy-duty mechanical piston briquetting presses to produce high-density biomass briquettes (white coal) for industrial boiler applications.

## 🏭 Process Flow & Technical Sizing

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Raw Wood Logs & Branches] --> B[Log-to-Sawdust Chipper / Shredder]
    B --> C[Screening & Hammer Mill]
    C --> D[Rotary Drum Dryer & Cyclone Train]
    D --> E[Dry Material Buffer Silos]
    E --> F[2x 90mm Piston Briquette Presses]
    F --> G[Cooling Conveyors & QC]
    G --> H[Finished Briquette Storage & Dispatch]
```

* **Operating Shifts:** 2 shifts/day × 8 hours = 16 hours/day (25 operating days/month).
* **Effective Run-Time:** 340 productive hours/month (at 85% plant availability).
* **Throughput Sizing:**
  * Raw Log Processing: ≥ 4.64 MT/hour effective requirement (designed for 5–6 TPH).
  * Moisture Evaporation: ~532 MT water evaporated/month (~1.56 MT/hour).
  * Finished Output: 2.94 MT/hour while running (serviced by two 1.3–2.0 TPH presses).
* **Connected Power:** ~380–420 kW; 500 kVA billing demand with 630 kVA HT transformer (11 kV HT Category III(A)).

## 📍 Strategic Location & Offtake Advantages

1. **Proximity to Industrial Boiler Demand:**
   * **Jawaharlal Nehru Pharma City (JNPC), Parawada (~25 km):** Major hub of bulk-drug active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) manufacturers with steady year-round high-pressure steam boilers (Hetero, Aurobindo, Eisai, Mylan).
   * **Atchutapuram SEZ / APIIC Industrial Parks (~15–20 km):** Textile, metallurgical, and chemical processing facilities replacing fossil coal and petcoke to meet APPCB emission standards.
2. **Timber Supply Chain:**
   * Abundant availability of fast-growing agro-forestry species in coastal Andhra Pradesh (*Casuarina equisetifolia / Sarugudu*, *Eucalyptus*, *Subabul*) which are exempted from rigid forest transit permits under the *A.P. Wood Based Industries Rules, 2018*.
