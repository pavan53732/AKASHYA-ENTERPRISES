### Engineering gate
At 25 operating days × 16 hours × 85% effective uptime: 340 productive hours/month; two 90-mm press lines each must continuously demonstrate ≥1.471 TPH; log preparation requires ≥4.64 wet TPH; dryer requires ~1.563 TPH **water evaporation**, with reserve to be proven in trial. A “2 TPH dryer” by *feed weight* is not sufficient evidence.
