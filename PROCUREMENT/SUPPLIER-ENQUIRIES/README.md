## Handling quotations and replies

- Verify the business contact, GSTIN/legal seller and current offer; use the short message for routing and full email for itemised response. Ask for PDF letterhead and Excel/CSV technical tables if available. Keep each quote's reference/date/validity and exact model revision, price and inclusion/exclusion scope.
- Maintain [supplier tracker](../SUPPLIER-CONTACTS-AND-RFQ-TRACKER.md) with actual sent/acknowledged/received status and [quote register](../2026-11-22-MACHINERY-PRICE-AND-RFQ-REGISTER.md). Record no status change without evidence. Compare [machine master](../MACHINERY-MASTER.csv) and [bid equalisation](../TECHNICAL-COMMERCIAL-BID-EQUALISATION.md); never mark catalogue or seller ask as written quotation.
- The existing detailed project-specific turnkey RFQ is an internal technical basis only. Do not send it or its repository link during this preliminary quotation collection stage without explicit owner authorisation.
- This repository is public. Do not upload original supplier quotes containing personal details, bank data, signatures or sensitive commercial material without owner approval; retain original evidence privately and update the sanitised register. No budgets or financial assumptions changed by these drafts.
