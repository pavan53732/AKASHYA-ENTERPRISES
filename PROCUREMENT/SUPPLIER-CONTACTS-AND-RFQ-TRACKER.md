## Questions to ask at first contact
1. Can you sign off **at least 1.60 MT/h saleable 90-mm wood briquettes per press** for 340 productive hours/month and fully specified wood?
2. Is the motor power **89.5 HP (EcoStan BPM90HD)** or **117 HP (EcoStan BM90/100 listing)**? Which exact commercial model and net rated performance will be supplied?
3. For 50% fresh logs, who guarantees **>5.57 wet TPH wood intake** and **2,492 kg water/h removal** at 340h? Which options quote >=3,000 kg/h evaporation with reserve?
4. Is 90-mm output sustained at the supplier-prescribed press-feed moisture and particle size? Who bears reject/wetwood trial risk?
5. Can seller provide **A.P. / Telangana service reference**, a witnessed 72-hour acceptance test and written spare prices?
6. What are actual monthly kWh, dryer heating fuel and total installed ex-GST costs to **Anakapalli**?
7. How many **physical ram strokes/min** are instrumented under load, vs shaft/crank RPM? State the vendor-required **cooling rest (if any)** after 8/16/20/24h continuous operation, and actual OEM shut-down minutes for die/knife/airlock/bearings.
8. For 40–50% greenwood can the vendor guarantee **all wood <8mm before IFD dryer**? Does wet-rated machine avoid the **≤15%-moisture** limitation of dry hammer mills? Show pre-dryer wet screen and dry stream **≥3.08TPH**.
9. Does 150,000kWh/month over 340 productive hours imply an unrealistic **441kW average** relative to full motor nameplate? Quote 630/800/1000kVA feeder alternatives and actual peak kW, starting current and power cost.
