## Required guaranteed process duty for 1,000 finished MT/month
- Schedules: compare **16h × 25d @85% availability** (340 productive hours; two presses each ≥1.471 TPH), **20h × 25d @85%** (425h; ≥1.176 TPH each), **24h × 26d @80%** (~499h; ≥1.002 TPH each).
- Log intake at 40% moisture: **1,579 MT/month**; ≥4.64 wet MT/hour for 340 effective hours; choose >=5–6 TPH actual performance with reserve. One “up to 5 TPH” ESM150 has narrow headroom. Provide at least one contingency option.
- Dryer water evaporation: calculated **~1,564 kg/hour** at 40% feed and two-shift schedule. Request **≥1,800–2,000 kg water/hour** *guaranteed* plus actual feed/dry tonnes and power/fuel requirement at incoming 40–50% wet-basis moisture. A “2 TPH dryer” without an evaporation guarantee is NON-COMPLIANT.
- Trial feed: legally sourced local Casuarina / Eucalyptus / Subabul logs or branches, with diameter distribution, bark/contamination, moisture, particle size and target 90mm briquettes measured.
- Motor connected load (~380–420 kW) is **not** monthly kWh; demand & transformer sizing are subject to electrical load schedule and APEPDCL feasibility.

## Supplier RFP acceptance criteria
1. Fixed battery limits and exclusions: conveyors/hoppers, magnetic separator, hammer mill, spark detector, dust collection, guards, PLC, fans, coolers, cranes, pipe/duct design, utility connections, civil interface, freight and insurance.
2. **72-hour witnessed performance test** at site (or comparable agreed prolonged duty), log moisture and wood species documented, output ≥40 tonnes/day during contracted schedule subject to approved maintenance downtime and acceptance protocol.
3. Separate rated and measured kWh/finished MT, thermal fuel kg/MT, die/wear part lives, hourly water evaporation, dry output moisture, briquette density/ash/GCV, reject rate.
4. Written service SLA, critical spare inventory and price locks, bearing/die/ram rework times, commissioning and safety obligations, response in Anakapalli, risk allocation.
5. Milestone payments only after agreed evidence; warranties and refund provisions subject to legal review; no promise that a 15 March bank disbursement will coincide with manufacturing commissioning.

## Why quotes differ
A ₹13 lakh stand-alone dryer listing cannot be compared to a ₹75 lakh **furnace + drying loop + cyclones + controls + spark/filtration + installation** engineering allowance. Demand identical scope and guaranteed duty before choosing any cheaper number.

## Amendment 22 Nov 2026: 50%-moisture performance alternative — mandatory quote
In addition to the 40% standard case, require **separate written price and operating guarantees for 45% and 50% incoming moisture** under the same finished production target. For 50% wet wood and 340 productive hours, indicative intake needs **5.57 wet TPH**, dryer evaporation **2,492 kg/hour** (prefer bidder to quote **~2,750–3,000 kg/hour** if year-round 50%-moisture design is desired), with wood species and final press-feed moisture specified. Ask each supplier to quote **(A) pre-seasoned wood + 2,000 kg/hr evaporative dryer** and **(B) as-received wetwood + larger dryer**, with capital/thermal/electricity and site footprint costs. Require absolute guarantees of water removed, kWh per saleable tonne, dryer thermal fuel kg/tonne and demonstrated tonnes of 90 mm briquettes per operating day. Price fields remain **UNKNOWN until binding quotes arrive**; online listings do not establish installed or tested plant cost.

Request chipper actual output at both 40% and 50% wood, handling of 10–12 inch or actual measured logs, bark and sand contamination, blade life/maintenance cost and alternative wetwood sorting/disposal. Include **boiler trial compatible fuel specs** (binderless 90mm, acceptable breakage and fines) and safe dust/fire design in battery limits.
