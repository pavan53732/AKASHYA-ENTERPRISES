### Critical new operating-cost question
The manufacturer technical data describes **up to about 440 kg thermal fuel/h (IFD3000) and 700 kg/h (IFD5000)** under specified reference fuel and thermal duty. **These are not predicted consumption at AKASHYA's actual loads**. At 340 productive hours/month, they correspond to *maximum-design reference quantities* of ~149.6 and ~238 MT/month if operated at those rates; the current OPEX reserves only ₹600 per finished MT / ₹6L monthly for dryer fuel. Require **measured kg of furnace fuel/kg water evaporated, actual fuel grade/cost and whether saleable briquettes are burned in the furnace**. Do not multiply catalog maximum by market sell price and claim actual costs; these scenarios must be trial-backed.
Source: [Infinite dryer technical page](https://www.infinitedryer.com/ifd.html), [PDF brochure](https://www.infiniteenergyindia.com/pdf/InfiniteDryers.pdf).

- **Current owner BOM:** ₹310L machinery/handling + ₹210L site/civil/utilities = **₹520L fixed**, plus **₹99.43L estimated opening working capital** = ~₹619.43L excluding land. These are planning budgets, not bank-approved capex.
- **Research-only broad inferred fixed CAPEX range:** ~**₹4.0–5.5 crore excluding land** based on limited public line-items and numerous unpriced interfaces. **LOW CONFIDENCE: NOT a bottom-up installed-price total, NOT a supplier offer, NOT an approved revision**. - For a **2 × Lehra** press public-price alternative, gross asking difference from 2 × EcoStan BM is ~₹8.4L; for Infinite IFD5000 vs IFD3000 asking difference is ~₹26L. **Neither difference can be directly netted from ₹520L** without reconciling different budget scope and equipment specification.
- **Customer acceptance and wood-seasonality tests** must precede a single final machine selection. Finance sheet sensitivity and 15-year replacement planning should only adopt signed vendor figures.

## E. Evidence hierarchy and expiry controls
**Primary OEM model datasheet** confirms a maker's *published* design claim, not operation on our wood. **Dated public seller listing** confirms an observed asking price, not a contract. **Written signed offer** with seller GSTIN, exact specification, cost inclusions, validity and authority is an actual supplier quote; it is **binding only to the extent its legal terms make it binding**, typically after purchase-order acceptance. **Site-witnessed acceptance trial** is strongest duty evidence. Save original URL, screenshots/exports when lawful, contact details, quote number, exchange/date, and internal reviewer. Recheck prices before bank filing and again before PO.
