**Illustrative production arithmetic:** **1,000/340=2,941 kg finished/h**, **1,471 kg/h each press** if both share equally; procurement **≥1,600 kg/h each sustained** gives about **1,088 MT/mo theoretical at 340h** (8.8% margin, not guaranteed). At **230 confirmed physical strokes/min** (hypothetical only), a 1,471 kg/h stream would require about **107 g per stroke**; at **216 physically confirmed strokes/min** it would require **114 g per stroke**. These are unit-conversion examples **NOT** claimed stroke mass, stroke count or production guarantees. Finished briquette length, density and die friction make units per stroke unproven. Do not buy a machine based on RPM or maximum advertisement alone.

**Press wear / running cycle, preliminary maintenance allowance [ENGINEERING ESTIMATE, NOT OEM POLICY]:** trained operator monitors continuously; sample feed moisture and output at least hourly, inspect lubrication alarms/bearing temperature/current every **2–4 running hours** without opening guards; plan **10–20 min/shift** recorded safe inspections plus **30–60 min/day** for agreed line housekeeping; **2–4 h weekly** for die/ram/bearing/fastener service; **4–8 h/month** for deeper checks. This is **planning time**, not a compulsory 1h stop every X run hours or a warranty on die lifetime. The assumed **85% availability already includes planned and unplanned losses**: do not subtract these times a second time. Stops, cooling and lockout are mandatory when the particular OEM interlock/manual requires them. Historical FAO literature suggests wear parts could last **~500–1,300 hours** under very different feed materials—**not a promise for modern EcoStan/Lehra/Jay/Ronak presses**; acquire cost *per finished tonne* and real-wood trial wear rate [FAO](https://www.fao.org/4/t0275e/t0275e04.htm).

## B. Dryer, furnace and feed preparation — compare actual water, dry output and incoming size
Infinite's **IFD series** lists water removal **AND** dried-product output. **Both** must clear the AKASHYA whole-line balance, and **input particle size <8 mm** is mandatory on that specific OEM page. The same page says optimal inlet moisture **<45%** in its description, a separate table heading **<50%**; therefore the boundary **exactly 50% moisture is NOT guaranteed** by its brochure. Incoming log chips of 20–30mm cannot simply be dumped into that flash dryer. Pre-reduction of **WET 40–50% wood to <8 mm** may be required; many dry-only hammer mills, including EcoStan's published HM family, allow **≤15%** moisture, so a generic HM must **not** be placed ahead of the dryer without a successful wet-wood test. Alternative: log-to-sawdust machine yielding enough <8mm fines, dedicated wet-rated mill, pre-seasoned sawdust, or a **rotary drying first stage** accepting larger chips. This is currently a **plant design HOLD POINT**, not a solved detail.

**Mass-flow design @340 productive hours, idealised and excluding handling rejects:**
- At **40% wet basis**: log receiving **4.64 wet MT/h** → water removed **1.563 MT/h** → roughly **3.08 MT/h material after primary drying** (depending how 5% solids loss is allocated) → 2.941 MT/h saleable output. Dryer and hammer mill must BOTH be sized for material flow, and press for finished flow.
- At **50%**: logs **5.57 wet MT/h**, water removed **2.492 MT/h** → roughly **3.08 MT/h post-dryer material**. Do not size *downstream* hammer mill at 5.57 wet TPH if it is installed *after drying*, but do not use a ≤15%-moisture mill **before** drying.
- Heat/fuel: Infinite publishes **<440 kg/h IFD3000** and **<700 kg/h IFD5000** for **<20%-moisture, >3,800 kcal/kg dry briquette/wood fuel**. If actually used at those upper reference quantities over 340h, these would mean **<149.6 vs <238 MT/month** fuel respectively, not guaranteed actual consumption. Existing **₹6L/month** fuel budget could be badly wrong depending purchased/captive feedstock fuel prices and real thermal duty. No claim of profitability at 50% is valid without a measured fuel curve.
- OEM explicitly **excludes dust collector, chimney beyond 30ft, civil/foundations, electrical cabling, GST and transportation**. Therefore **₹42L and ₹68L are NOT complete installed dryer/furnace/dust-safe line prices**.

**Working dryer+furnace package estimates [ENGINEERING]:** base 40% case ~**₹65–105L**, high-moisture 50% duty ~**₹95–145L**, depending furnace, ducts, thermal controls and actual water trials. **Do not double count ₹20–40L baghouse/fire controls** in the separate safety budget below. The existing owner's **₹75L** was a base planning package, not year-round wet-wood guarantee.

**Service allowances [ENGINEERING, NOT OEM]:** bag filter ΔP and hot-gas O₂/temperature logging every running hour; cyclone and spark collection every shift; burner ash removal and rotary-airlock inspection at approved safe shutdown; **10–30 min per shift** inspection allowance, **2–4h weekly**, **4–8h/month**, all included in 85% general availability. Hazard detection/LOTO and contractor-approved isolation decide whether a stop is needed, not a universal cool-off after X hours.

### Power sizing caution
Official examples are **89.5 HP ×2** presses, **159 HP ESM150**, **133–190 HP** one dry-rated HM, **67 HP IFD3000** (or **136 HP IFD5000**), plus fans, feeder motors, baghouse and loader charging as applicable. These motor nameplates total **~538–595 HP (401–444 kW)** *before* auxiliaries in the 40%-wood example; **630 kVA transformer sufficiency is therefore unproven** and starting-current/demand diversity must be assessed by licensed electrical engineer. A 10TPH 250–300HP log eater could raise peak connected demand further. Historic owner **150 kWh/finished MT** is an unverified *energy usage assumption*, not a consequence of adding nameplate HP. Need supplier measured **kWh/finished MT** and AP electricity quotation.

## E. Evidence hierarchy and expiry controls
**Primary OEM model datasheet** confirms a maker's *published* design claim, not operation on our wood. **Dated public seller listing** confirms an observed asking price, not a contract. **Written signed offer** with seller GSTIN, exact specification, cost inclusions, validity and authority is an actual supplier quote; it is **binding only to the extent its legal terms make it binding**, typically after purchase-order acceptance. **Site-witnessed acceptance trial** is strongest duty evidence. Save original URL, screenshots/exports when lawful, contact details, quote number, exchange/date, and internal reviewer. Recheck prices before bank filing and again before PO.

**BPM and stoppages:** Vendor mechanical 216/230 RPM numbers do **not** certify actual reciprocating strokes/min, guaranteed kg/stroke, permissible uninterrupted shifts, mandatory cool-down time, or die lifetime. Require OEM manuals and a 72 logged running-hour acceptance trial. The 85% plant availability assumption already includes planned inspection windows; buyer should not double-deduct them.
