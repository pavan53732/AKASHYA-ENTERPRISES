## B. Dryer, furnace and feed preparation — compare actual water, dry output and incoming size
Infinite's **IFD series** lists water removal **AND** dried-product output. **Both** must clear the AKASHYA whole-line balance, and **input particle size <8 mm** is mandatory on that specific OEM page. The same page says optimal inlet moisture **<45%** in its description, a separate table heading **<50%**; therefore the boundary **exactly 50% moisture is NOT guaranteed** by its brochure. Incoming log chips of 20–30mm cannot simply be dumped into that flash dryer. Pre-reduction of **WET 40–50% wood to <8 mm** may be required; many dry-only hammer mills, including EcoStan's published HM family, allow **≤15%** moisture, so a generic HM must **not** be placed ahead of the dryer without a successful wet-wood test. Alternative: log-to-sawdust machine yielding enough <8mm fines, dedicated wet-rated mill, pre-seasoned sawdust, or a **rotary drying first stage** accepting larger chips. This is currently a **plant design HOLD POINT**, not a solved detail.

### Power sizing caution
Official examples are **89.5 HP ×2** presses, **159 HP ESM150**, **133–190 HP** one dry-rated HM, **67 HP IFD3000** (or **136 HP IFD5000**), plus fans, feeder motors, baghouse and loader charging as applicable. These motor nameplates total **~538–595 HP (401–444 kW)** *before* auxiliaries in the 40%-wood example; **630 kVA transformer sufficiency is therefore unproven** and starting-current/demand diversity must be assessed by licensed electrical engineer. A 10TPH 250–300HP log eater could raise peak connected demand further. Historic owner **150 kWh/finished MT** is an unverified *energy usage assumption*, not a consequence of adding nameplate HP. Need supplier measured **kWh/finished MT** and AP electricity quotation.

## E. Evidence hierarchy and expiry controls
**Primary OEM model datasheet** confirms a maker's *published* design claim, not operation on our wood. **Dated public seller listing** confirms an observed asking price, not a contract. **Written signed offer** with seller GSTIN, exact specification, cost inclusions, validity and authority is an actual supplier quote; it is **binding only to the extent its legal terms make it binding**, typically after purchase-order acceptance. **Site-witnessed acceptance trial** is strongest duty evidence. Save original URL, screenshots/exports when lawful, contact details, quote number, exchange/date, and internal reviewer. Recheck prices before bank filing and again before PO.

**BPM and stoppages:** Vendor mechanical 216/230 RPM numbers do **not** certify actual reciprocating strokes/min, guaranteed kg/stroke, permissible uninterrupted shifts, mandatory cool-down time, or die lifetime. Require OEM manuals and a 72 logged running-hour acceptance trial. The 85% plant availability assumption already includes planned inspection windows; buyer should not double-deduct them.
