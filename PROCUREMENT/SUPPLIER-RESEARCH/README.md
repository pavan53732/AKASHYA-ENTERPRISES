## Machinery-by-machinery comparison

## Required review sequence

1. Read the brand's exact existing entries and any written quotation; verify legal entity, official navigation, model tables, FAQs, technical pages, documents and useful demonstrations. Scope to clean wood briquettes; mark pellet-only and unrelated equipment separately.
2. Catalogue inventory by stage and model, rated throughput basis, HP and individual motor specs, input/output size and moisture, mechanism and usage, dimensions, controls, ancillary scope, compatibility and wear components. Record sources and dates; use unknown for missing facts.
3. Separate catalogue, historical, marketplace, written quotation and tested performance; audit conflicts rather than overwrite. Assess internal stage matching, conditional versus proven duty, operating costs and service. No unverified cost or purchase decision.
4. Make a supplier-specific **known / unknown / discrepancy / exact question** register, including real production, loaded BPM/RPM, actual kWh/t and fuel, uptime/rest, maintenance, spares and price/scope, service and testing. Tailor only that brand's existing external draft, keeping internal information out.
5. Update the shared master, price register and relevant existing machinery/BOM files where evidence changes; check model uniqueness, price totals, links and source classification. Record review status and proceed to the next brand only after completing the present public review. Actual vendor reply can remain pending and be reconciled later; public review is not technical approval.

Follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Do not overwrite prior verified financial baselines, root workbook or PDF without evidence and binary checks.
