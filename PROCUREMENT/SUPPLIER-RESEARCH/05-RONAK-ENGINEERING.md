## 7. Internal project-fit screening — never disclose

For 1,000 MT/month over 340 productive hours (`≥4.64 / 5.57 wet TPH`, `1,563 / 2,492 kg/h` water evaporation, `~3.08 dry TPH`, `2.941` combined / `≥1.60 TPH` per press target):
- **`Jumbo 90` (`93.5 HP`, `1.2–1.5 TPH` current OEM):** **Fails internal ≥1.60 TPH/press acceptance target on catalogue maximum** (`1.50 TPH × 2 × 340 h = 1,020 MT` theoretical ceiling, leaving just 2% headroom and **zero** margin for maintenance/rejects). Treat as a price/technical comparator unless Ronak delivers verified hardwood test output `≥1.60 TPH` and reconciles `93.5 / 91 / 88 HP` variants.
- **Chippers (`64 HP` `0.8–1.0 TPH`, `HDWC-30` `1.0–1.5 TPH`):** **Far undersized** (~3–5× below `4.64 / 5.57 wet TPH` single-stream log intake); `25–100 cm` log spec on `HDWC-30` is a glaring typo (`mm`, not `cm`).
- **Dryers (`39 HP` / `30–40 HP` Flash vs `88 HP` Rotary):** Flash units (`0.8–2.0 TPH` dry output, zero evaporation curves) are **too small** for full 1,000 MT/month stream (`~3.08 TPH` dry) and unverified at `50%` moisture; **`88 HP Rotary Drum Dryer`** (`₹48.00L` TrustSEAL) has **no published capacity, moisture limit, evaporation kg/h, or drum size** and does not appear on `ronakeng.com` at all.
