## Gate 3 — bank case and long-horizon ownership
After Gate 1 is passed, financial reviewer models: **installed capex + opening working capital + borrowing conditions + interest/moratorium + DSCR + 15-year price escalation and replacement**. Compute discount-weighted lifetime cost using actual electricity, dryer fuel, wear and downtime. A cheaper press with a marginal sustained 1.5 TPH output may lose more revenue than its capex saving.

**Do not award to lowest price** without technical compliance, written quotation and ownership/maintenance costs; preserve owner target first eligible loan disbursement by **15 Mar 2027** as conditional.
