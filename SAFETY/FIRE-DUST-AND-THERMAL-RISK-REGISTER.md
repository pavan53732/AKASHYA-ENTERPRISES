# Preliminary safety and operational risk register — wood briquette manufacturing
**Prepared 22 Nov 2026.** This is *not* a design certificate or substitute for site-specific statutory fire/industrial-safety assessment. Engage a competent local fire, electrical, process and structural engineer. Imported OSHA references are technical learning, **not direct claims of Andhra Pradesh legal applicability**.

| Risk | How it can occur | Engineering / management requirement | Evidence to close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood-dust fire/explosion | Drying, transfer, baghouse or airborne fine dust and ignition sources | Engineer-designed dust extraction, spark detection, suitable explosion protection/isolation/venting, housekeeping, protected electrical systems | Combustible-dust risk assessment and OEM designs |
| Dryer over-temperature, smouldering | Wet chip variations, entrained embers, stalled fan and fuel stoker malfunction | Interlocked temperatures/airflow, controlled hot shutdown, emergency response, OEM-approved safe-fire protocol | Signed cause-and-effect logic, witnessed test |
| Excess feed moisture to mechanical piston press | Dryer failure or wet material bypass | OEM moisture range + in-line batch sampling/automatic diversion and guarded press operation | OEM acceptance and tested alarm trip |
| Chipper projectile/foreign metal | Mixed branches, stones, wires, nails and loader misfeed | Debris exclusion, guards, magnetic/metal rejection, safe loading, isolation and maintenance lockout | OEM drawing and training record |
| Manual fuel feeding at customer's boiler | Backfire, hot-door exposure, excess air and feed-rate swings | Buyer utilities/EHS decide safe feeding and fuel dimensions; no assumption 90 mm is accepted | Buyer trial report |
| Hot finished briquettes | Press discharge/long cooling channels can burn material/people | Safe guarded cooling, hot-particle detection, proper storage after cooling | Temperature/containment trial |
| Open chips & sawdust heaps | Self-heating, rain moisture, ignition and dust clouds | Storage height/turnover/stockpile fire controls per engineer | Fire risk review and traffic layout |
| Night operation | Fatigue, under-staffing and unsafe maintenance | Legal shifts/rest, qualified operators/supervisors, no unattended hot dryer | Approved roster and SOP |
| Electrical fault | High-power motors, VFDs and HT gear | APEPDCL-approved capacity, earth/leakage/short circuit protection, authorised electricians | Electrical drawings/certificates |
| Fuel supply failure / poor wood quality | Unlicensed wood, wet loads, treated timber | Legal source, weigh/moisture acceptance, untreated-wood-only | Supplier certificates and traceability |

## Safe procurement conditions
**Never accept unsupported declarations** that a "cyclone + baghouse" alone constitutes full fire/explosion compliance. Capture design standards/permit conditions, maintenance and isolation testing. Allocate CAPEX line-item contingencies based on vendor-engineered rather than generic ₹25 lakh allowances.
