# Fifteen-year owner operating and inflation model — AKASHYA ENTERPRISES
**Version:** 2026-11-22 | **Horizon:** 15 operating years after commissioning, NOT a guaranteed 15-year bank-loan tenure. Currency: nominal INR; tables in **₹ lakh** except price/tonne. Excludes land acquisition, land lease, finance interest, principal, taxes, input GST working-capital timing and residual value. No bank sanction or buyer agreement is assumed.

## Decision-critical conclusion
- 1,000 MT/month is the **monthly saleable design target**, not output guaranteed in every month.
- At full run rate today: selling ₹8,000/MT, wet wood ₹2,000/MT, 40% incoming moisture, 10% finished moisture, 5% dry-solids loss, ₹68.50 lakh/month indicative OPEX, **₹11.50 lakh/month EBITDA** (not net profit).
- A 15-year fixed ₹8,000 selling price is unsafe. Independently model buyer-price escalation, raw wood escalation and electricity/wages. Do not use general CPI alone as a proxy for these commodity inputs.
- **Model outcome:** base scenario (+3%/year sale, +6% wood, +5% power and other, +6% salaries) turns negative at year 7; even the apparently profitable first-year cost structure can erode over time.
- Under the illustrative +5% annual sales-price scenario with the same cost inflation, the cumulative unlevered post-replacement project cash crosses the initial ₹6.1943 crore around year 8. This is **not** a financial IRR or debt-service forecast and assumes continuous 15-year operation.

Fixed-monthly at full base schedule: payroll ₹8.50L, administration ₹2.70L, power demand/adjustments ₹2.975L, and **half** the ₹2.50L maintenance allowance (₹1.25L) = ₹15.425L/month. All fixed items incur 12 months even in production ramp; variable items follow tonnes sold.

**Method:** yearly sales = output × independently indexed price; raw-wood cost = output × wet-wood ratio × independently indexed delivered-wood price; each variable OPEX stream escalates under its own factor; fixed power and overheads incur throughout the year, even at partial utilisation. EBITDA is before depreciation/interest/taxes. Annual cash after replacement capital = EBITDA − scheduled replacement CAPEX. Cumulative illustrative project cash starts at **−₹619.43 lakh** (₹520L initial fixed + ₹99.43L opening WC); changes in WC, GST, debt repayments, tax and salvage proceeds are NOT included, so the cumulative column is only an indicator.

## 15-year trading policy to preserve margins
1. **Customer contracts:** negotiate monthly/quarterly price reset linked to a documented weighted basket of wet timber, electricity and logistics costs; include a documented floor/ceiling mechanism only where acceptable to both parties. No unilateral escalation assumed.
2. **Feedstock:** seek 12-month staggered supply agreements, moisture-based weighment/rejection, legal source proof, at least two suppliers/species, and a maximum landed wet-wood procurement price tied to current sale contracts.
3. **Quarterly investment committee review:** report rolling 90-day realised ex-GST sales prices, weighted raw price, moisture, actual material balance, specific kWh/MT, dryer fuel/MT, buyer days-sales-outstanding, gross margin and 12-month projected cash coverage.
4. **Stop/go trigger:** do not lock large equipment deposits until 3 independent vendor technical/price bids, bank term sheet, written wood supply proof and buyer trial orders/offtake evidence exist.
5. **Reset after commissioning:** replace all preliminary estimates with 90-day actual operation, revise ramp, replacement costs, tax/loan schedule and future WC needs.

## Important scope definitions
- **15 years** here means operating/asset-study horizon, *not* a promised 15-year loan or a lease that is automatically valid for 15 years.
- **Deadline 15 March 2027** in [PROJECT-ROADMAP.md](../PROJECT-ROADMAP.md) is a **target first lender disbursement**, not a guaranteed commissioning or sanction date.
- CPI inflation is **not** the assumed growth rate for timber or briquettes. Drivers are separately modelled as volatile commercial prices.
- The existing PDF and XLSX in repository are earlier owner-planning snapshots; this 15-year module is the current **scenario supplement**, not a substitute for a banker-certified financial model.

## Moisture variability — explicit 15-year stress assumption (new 22 Nov 2026)
The 15-year tables above **hold incoming wood moisture at 40%**. They model **commodity price escalation** but **not changing seasonal moisture, species mixture, dryer duty, per-kg evaporation heat, or fire/dust overhaul cost**. They therefore must not be described as a complete 15-year physical plant model. For each operating year and quarterly wet/dry procurement mix, separately input measured wood moisture, wood intake factor, required dryer water removal, true thermal fuel consumption and capacity-shortfall penalties. At constant 1,000MT/month, 50% wet wood increases purchased tonnes from 1,579 to 1,895 and water evaporated from 532 to 847 MT monthly. **If output becomes dryer constrained, revenue also falls; do not assume sales remain 1,000 MT.** Link a properly validated engineering scenario to the workbook after supplier testing. Engineering table: [moisture model](../MACHINERY/05-WOOD-MOISTURE-AND-DRYER-SIZING.md). Buyer fuel-type risk: [boiler trial](../MARKET-RESEARCH/03-90MM-BOILER-COMPATIBILITY-TRIAL-PROTOCOL.md).
