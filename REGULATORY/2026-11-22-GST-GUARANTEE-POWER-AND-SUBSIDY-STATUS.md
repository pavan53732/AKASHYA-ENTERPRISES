## CGTMSE — credit guarantee is not an automatic loan
Government MSME credit guarantee rules effective 1 April 2025 allow **eligible bank credit facilities up to ₹10 crore** to be considered under the CGTMSE scheme. The cap is a **maximum eligible facility / guarantee framework**, not an entitlement to a ₹10 crore loan, guarantee of interest rate, 15-year loan tenor, or promise of disbursement by 15 March 2027. Lender still appraises project and decides security, promoter contribution, guarantees, collateral or hybrid cover, fees, turnover, DSCR, credit history and bank policy. Record written scheme eligibility and sanction conditions by the chosen lender.
Primary sources: https://www.cgtmse.in/Default/ViewFile/?id=1743176302611_CGTMSE+-+Scheme+Document+CGS+I_updated+as+on+Apr+1+2025.pdf&path=Page ; https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=84 ; https://dcmsme.gov.in/CLCS_TUS_Scheme/Credit_Guarantee_Scheme/Scheme_Guidelines.aspx .

## BioURJA MNRE — no subsidy assumed
MNRE's National Bioenergy Programme information describes Phase I duration ending **31 March 2026**. BioURJA portal states biomass briquette/pellet proposals received **from 10 Oct 2024** will be considered after approval of Phase II budget. This is **not** a confirmed current entitlement for AKASHYA ENTERPRISES and is modelled as **₹0 until formal approval**. Seek written eligibility and availability on the application date, not merely screenshots of historical rates.
Sources: https://biourja.mnre.gov.in/about-the-programme ; https://biourja.mnre.gov.in/ ; https://mnre.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/biomass-programme-kDNxIjMtQWa .

## Electricity — APEPDCL and APERC FY 2026–27
APERC FY 2026–27 industrial 11kV HT reference shows normal-period **₹6.30/kVAh** and **₹475/kVA/month** demand charges (time-of-day, billing demand, power factor, taxes/charges may alter actual bill). Plant owner model uses 150,000 kWh/month and an assumed 500 kVA billing demand, not a utility quotation. **At 20/24-hour shift patterns, peak versus normal/off-peak energy mix changes** and power bill cannot be held automatically at ₹12.92 lakh. Obtain dated APEPDCL feasibility, sanctioned CMD, approved transformer and detailed hourly demand/energy calculator.
Primary: https://aperc.gov.in/page/Tariff_Orders ; https://apeasternpower.com/ .

## Product GST and HSN
CBIC GST goods-rate schedule lists **wood chips, sawdust and wood waste/scrap agglomerated into briquettes/pellets under HSN 4401** in the **5% GST** bracket at checked rate. This is indicative for *this specified wood-waste product description*. **CA must confirm exact classification of log-derived wood fuel, applicable product subheading and latest notification before the first tax invoice.** All model revenue/costs are **ex GST**; model input credit and cash timing separately.
Primary: https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-goods-services-rates.html ; https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/gst-goods-services-rates.html .
