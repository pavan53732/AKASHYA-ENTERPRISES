# Land, licensing, legal gates and 15-year tenure
**Location:** Anakapalli district, Andhra Pradesh. **No private land acquisition cost** in current project budget; site is NOT yet verified or approved.

## Three different tenures — don't confuse
1. **Business horizon:** 15 years of commercial operations for strategy, machinery replacements and price-index forecasts.
2. **Land/site legal control:** aim for a secure registered lease/ownership arrangement compatible with the long project horizon, lender security and renewal clauses; a proposed 15-year lease does not exist until executed.
3. **Loan tenor:** bank-negotiated principal repayment period and moratorium; **never assume** banks provide 15-year industrial term loans.

## Dependency checklist
| Gate | Who / evidence | Hard rule |
|---|---|---|
| Timber species and wood-processing/transit permission | Divisional Forest Officer written ruling, legal source records, transport permissions as applicable | No unsupported assertion that all plantation logs are exempt |
| Site land use/building | Local planning/industrial authority, legal counsel, site plan | No construction prior to required approvals |
| APPCB CTE | https://apocmms.nic.in/APPCB/ | Written permit and conditions before installation when legally required |
| APPCB CTO | APPCB, after successful installation and testing | Before regulated commercial operations |
| Factory registration/licence, labour | Competent AP authority; updated current-law requirements | Design proper shifts/rest; 20 h/day cannot mean illegal 10-h routine shifts |
| Fire and combustible dust | Fire Services plus qualified fire/dust specialist | Evaluate explosion isolation, safe silos, spark detection, earthing, fire access and prevention |
| 11 kV connection | **APEPDCL** site feasibility, approved load/SLD | 630 kVA preliminary only, demand and kWh tariff based on sanctioned schedule |
| GST / Udyam | GST adviser and official Udyam | Likely **small**, subject to correct investment/turnover computation; do not claim medium |
| Groundwater / borewell | Relevant groundwater/water authority | Permit when triggered |

## Do not hard-code unconfirmed standards
Previous roadmap prescribed a 30-m chimney, fixed 50,000-litre fire water, and specific particulate limits based on analogy. They are **project-specific design/permit outputs**, not automatic universally applicable conditions. Engineer to actual fuel-firing thermal capacity, location and approved fire plan.

## 15-year environmental controls
Keep source traceability, species/moisture/ash test records, product chain of custody, emissions monitoring, EPR/fire compliance (if applicable), waste disposal registers, hazardous treated-wood exclusion and annually reviewed legal obligations.
