1. **Absolute deadline:** the owner's 15 March 2027 target is conditional **first bank disbursement**, not the Indian FY end (31 March) or equipment commissioning. Never represent approval as assured.
2. **Unit economics:** baseline ₹8,000 per finished MT and ₹2,000 per incoming *wet* MT are promoter-reported rates, **not signed 1,000 MT/month contracts**. Every output must identify price basis, GST/freight, moisture, date, volume and validity.

4. **Machinery:** BPM is model designation, *not* proven ram strokes per minute. Distinguish per press nameplate output, continuous tested output, installed kW, actual kWh/MT, dryer *kg water evaporated per hour* and wet feed TPH.
5. **Sources:** classify every number PROMOTER-OBSERVED / PRIMARY OFFICIAL / MANUFACTURER CLAIM / PUBLIC ASKING PRICE / DERIVED FORMULA / PLANNING ESTIMATE / UNKNOWN. Never call an online listing a binding quote.
6. **Scenario model:** keep separate sale price inflation, timber price inflation, power, wage, thermal fuel and other costs, varying utilisation and capital replacement, 15 operating years. Annual inflation assumptions are illustrative, not forward forecasts.
7. **Approval gate:** verify location-based APPCB, forest, APEPDCL, factory, fire and site permissions; no guaranteed subsidies or automatic CGTMSE sanction.
8. **Market:** historical boiler consent = potential capacity, not current consumption or buyer contract; preserve uncertainty on wood vs agro feedstock.
9. **Workbook/PDF:** binary financial snapshots in root may lag later Markdown research. Cross-check them explicitly; do not claim spreadsheet formulas tested without actual formula inspection. When updating XLSX, preserve formulas, recalc and verify Summary, CAPEX, Payroll and Scenarios.
10. **Safety:** do not suggest defeating interlocks, hot-dust transport without engineered controls, or unattended dryer operations.
11. Commit changes in coherent, documented groups and preserve photo sources; avoid modifying previous field evidence.

12. **Read supplied drawbacks research:** [critical source review](RESEARCH/2025-12-13-BIOMASS-BRIQUETTE-DRAWBACKS-CRITICAL-REVIEW.md). Distinguish pellet-mill observations from piston-press faults. Never universally promise 90 mm boiler compatibility, carbon neutrality or automatic carbon credits.

## Mandatory supplier-communication policy — preliminary quotation collection

**Owner-approved approach (25 November 2026): collect information first, independently evaluate machinery and economics, then decide configuration and negotiate.** This policy governs every supplier-facing email, WhatsApp message, contact form, RFQ, attachment, follow-up and AI-generated draft, including any future supplier. It takes precedence over internal technical targets, financing deadlines and the project-specific RFQ when preparing external correspondence. The owner will send the requests and collect the quotations; an agent must not send, submit, call or otherwise contact a supplier without separate explicit authorisation.

### Include / request

| Area | Required external content |
|---|---|
| Opening and identity | Professional, neutral preliminary technical/commercial enquiry; delivery region Anakapalli–Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, when needed for freight/service. Do not add buyer name, organisation, personal contact, exact site or signature details unless owner authorises; never invent them. |
| Existing supplier offer | Recipient's own quotation reference/date, model, revision, prices and known terms where relevant to a clarification. Do not expose another supplier's offer. |
| Research before writing | Read the supplier's official product pages, catalogues, FAQs, technical articles, manuals and existing repository model/price records. Request missing, contradictory, obsolete or unverifiable data rather than re-asking all published facts. Distinguish catalogue claims from measured performance and guarantees. |
| Machinery and prices | Current model-wise unit prices, exact model/revision, legal seller/OEM, GST, bare machine vs standard package, included/mandatory/optional accessories; ask for standard available configurations across capacities without specifying our intended plant size or purchase quantities. |
| Actual performance | Minimum/typical/maximum sustained saleable production by wood species; physical loaded piston strokes/min separately from shaft/crank/flywheel RPM, motor details, input/output particle sizes, moisture limits, rejection, quality, cooling and lubrication. Ask only for machinery the supplier offers; identify bought-out items. |
| Comparative conditions | Request performance across supported moisture levels (e.g. 15–50% wet basis and higher only where rated), common wood types, and 1/8/12/16/20/24-hour schedules, 25/26 working days, strictly as comparison cases, not our preferred design. Ask mass balance per one saleable tonne, not our monthly requirement. For dryers ask simultaneous wet feed, evaporation kg/h, dry output, outlet moisture, fuel and electricity under the same conditions. |
| Operations and cost | Metered kWh per running hour and tonne, furnace fuel per hour and tonne, mandatory stops/rest/cooling, start/stop, downtime, wear life, preventive maintenance, part-numbered spares prices, sharpening/repair/overhaul, replacement intervals, staffing and automation. |
| Complete scope | Stage-wise standard-package BOM, model and quantity as defined by supplier, capacity matching and bottlenecks, missing ancillary items, layouts, dimensions/foundations, electrical load, fire/dust protection and utilities. |
| Commercial and service | Ex-Works, GST, freight, insurance, delivered/installed costs, installation/commissioning, training, lead time, payment options, warranty, AMC, local service, spares delivery and long-term support. Ask for written quotation and FAT/SAT, acceptance criteria, actual wood trial, remedies and consented customer references. |
| Documentation | Existing manuals, drawings, technical data and measured test reports may be attached instead of repeating answers. Ask supplier to label tested/estimated/guaranteed/unavailable fields. |

### Do not disclose without specific owner approval

- Our internal production targets or planned scale (daily, monthly, annual or per-press target), intended quantities/number of lines, preferred machine/configuration, preferred shifts/operating schedule, uptime assumption, productive hours or internal bottleneck requirements.
- Our chosen raw material moisture design case, actual wood purchase quantities, supply commitments, internally calculated mass balances, required wet/dry throughput or evaporation duty. Neutral ranges and unit-based questions above are allowed.
- Our project investment budget, CAPEX/OPEX estimates, working capital, raw material purchase or briquette selling prices, profit margins, revenue, payback, cash flow, lender or bank details, funding/subsidy arrangements, internal deadlines and negotiation ceiling.
- Our planned purchases, urgency, readiness to order, factory commitments, buyer/customer identities, competitors' quotations, negotiated terms or competing supplier details. Do not misrepresent facts or fabricate a project stage; use truthful neutral wording.
- Internal DPR, workbook, scenario model, formal project-specific RFQ, internal BOM/targets, repository URLs, internal research files or screenshots, private supplier/customer information, bank details and personal identifiers. Do not send attachments or links that reveal restricted information indirectly.
