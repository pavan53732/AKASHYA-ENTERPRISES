# Instructions for all AI agents editing AKASHYA ENTERPRISES
Read [README.md](README.md), [PROJECT-ROADMAP.md](PROJECT-ROADMAP.md), [FINANCE/15-YEAR-INFLATION-AND-CASHFLOW-MODEL.md](FINANCE/15-YEAR-INFLATION-AND-CASHFLOW-MODEL.md), and [PROCUREMENT/2026-11-22-MACHINERY-PRICE-AND-RFQ-REGISTER.md](PROCUREMENT/2026-11-22-MACHINERY-PRICE-AND-RFQ-REGISTER.md) first.

1. **Absolute deadline:** the owner's 15 March 2027 target is conditional **first bank disbursement**, not the Indian FY end (31 March) or equipment commissioning. Never represent approval as assured.
2. **Unit economics:** baseline ₹8,000 per finished MT and ₹2,000 per incoming *wet* MT are promoter-reported rates, **not signed 1,000 MT/month contracts**. Every output must identify price basis, GST/freight, moisture, date, volume and validity.
3. **Land:** exclude land acquisition unless owner changes scope. Do not silently exclude factory civil/shed costs. Separate 15-year operating horizon, land site lease and debt amortisation tenor.
4. **Machinery:** BPM is model designation, *not* proven ram strokes per minute. Distinguish per press nameplate output, continuous tested output, installed kW, actual kWh/MT, dryer *kg water evaporated per hour* and wet feed TPH.
5. **Sources:** classify every number PROMOTER-OBSERVED / PRIMARY OFFICIAL / MANUFACTURER CLAIM / PUBLIC ASKING PRICE / DERIVED FORMULA / PLANNING ESTIMATE / UNKNOWN. Never call an online listing a binding quote.
6. **Scenario model:** keep separate sale price inflation, timber price inflation, power, wage, thermal fuel and other costs, varying utilisation and capital replacement, 15 operating years. Annual inflation assumptions are illustrative, not forward forecasts.
7. **Approval gate:** verify location-based APPCB, forest, APEPDCL, factory, fire and site permissions; no guaranteed subsidies or automatic CGTMSE sanction.
8. **Market:** historical boiler consent = potential capacity, not current consumption or buyer contract; preserve uncertainty on wood vs agro feedstock.
9. **Workbook/PDF:** binary financial snapshots in root may lag later Markdown research. Cross-check them explicitly; do not claim spreadsheet formulas tested without actual formula inspection. When updating XLSX, preserve formulas, recalc and verify Summary, CAPEX, Payroll and Scenarios.
10. **Safety:** do not suggest defeating interlocks, hot-dust transport without engineered controls, or unattended dryer operations.
11. Commit changes in coherent, documented groups and preserve photo sources; avoid modifying previous field evidence.
